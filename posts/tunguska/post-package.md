## 캡션 (인스타 · 쓰레드 공용)

> **What caused the Tunguska explosion of 1908?** The largest explosion in recorded
> history flattened 2,150 km² of Siberian forest — and never left an impact crater.
>
> On 30 June 1908, something burst in the sky above the Podkamennaya Tunguska River
> in Siberia. It knocked down an estimated 80 million trees, splayed outward from a
> single point like spokes on a wheel.
>
> The region was so remote that no scientist reached it for 19 years. When Leonid
> Kulik finally arrived in 1927, he was looking for a crater. He never found one.
>
> The reason: it never hit the ground. The object broke apart roughly 8 km up and
> dumped its energy into the air as a shockwave. At the very centre, trees were left
> standing — stripped of branches, but upright — because the blast came straight down
> on top of them.
>
> What's still open: nobody has ever recovered a fragment. Rock or ice, we don't know.
>
> Photographs: Kulik expedition, May 1929, and Sibirskaya Zhizn, July 1908. Public domain.
>

**쓰지 않은 것:** 에너지 규모(메가톤). 문헌마다 10~50Mt로 편차가 커서
한 숫자로 단정할 수 없습니다. 이런 건 카드에 안 씁니다.
